Useful checks before you go

  • Check both trains for the exact Saturday date in RailOne / NTES.
  • Confirm the return fare and the destination station.
  • Confirm the auto fare, waiting limit and pickup time in writing.
  • Carry water, snacks, sun protection and some cash.
  • Keep enough buffer to reach Chintamani station before the return train.
